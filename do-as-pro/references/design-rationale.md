# Design Rationale

## Why professional reframing is the entrypoint

A non-expert often knows the desired real-world outcome but cannot name the professional task, method, or acceptance standard. Large language models often contain enough general professional knowledge to propose these, but vague prompts tend to elicit generic averaged responses. The skill therefore requires AI to select and justify a professional task archetype before broad execution.

## Why requirements remain provisional

Professional reframing reduces user-side unknowns but does not eliminate model error, outdated knowledge, missing internal context, or empirical uncertainty. The current specification is versioned and may be revised when evidence reveals a specification or model defect.

## Why the process is not a full software-engineering copy

Software-engineering ideas such as modularity, interfaces, versioning, tests, regression control, and fault isolation are useful. A strict specification-first model is not sufficient because unfamiliar-domain work includes unknown unknowns, tacit professional standards, and problem definitions that emerge through examples and real-world contact.

## Why scientific testing is conditional

Some questions are empirical and should be converted into hypotheses and minimal tests. Others are factual, mathematical, normative, or rule-based and require different validation. The skill first classifies the claim, then selects the validation method.

## Why complexity is evidence-triggered

AI tends to add caveats, roles, scenarios, and checks defensively. This can produce a process that appears thorough but is difficult to execute and audit. The skill starts with a compact path and adds structure only when it addresses a named risk or defect.

## Why external similarity is a calibration tool, not a conformity rule

Comparison with expert artifacts, common ranges, and public facts can reveal implausible outputs. A deviation may still be valid when supported by different context, evidence, and explicit risk analysis. The method flags unexplained deviation rather than requiring conformity to the average.

## Why the skill is self-contained

The workflow may be applied across domains, so it must not depend on a domain router or another skill's hidden contract. Domain tools and sources may improve execution, but the orchestration rules, evidence types, state transitions, quality gates, revision logic, and delivery conditions remain inside `do-as-pro`. A missing specialized capability reduces what can be verified or executed; it does not make the method undefined.

## Why the product name is broader than the core mechanism

Professional reframing is the main breakthrough because it turns an ordinary goal into the right expert task. The complete skill also performs specification, incremental execution, review, calibration, experiment design, revision, and bounded delivery. `Do as Pro` names the full user outcome while retaining professional reframing as the entry mechanism.
