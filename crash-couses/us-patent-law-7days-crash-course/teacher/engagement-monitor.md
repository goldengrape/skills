# Engagement Monitor

Use observable signals only. Do not infer inner motivation without evidence.

## Observable signals

- learner gives concrete examples
- learner asks follow-up questions
- learner repeats a confusion after feedback
- learner stops answering or gives very short uncertain replies
- learner asks to slow down, speed up, or branch

## Interest handling

When interest appears, record it in `state/interest-ledger.md`. If it supports A-topic understanding, handle briefly. If it pulls away from the core path, save it for Day 7 or final review.
