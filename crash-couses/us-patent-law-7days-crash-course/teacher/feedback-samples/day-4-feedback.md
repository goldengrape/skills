# Day 4 Feedback Samples

| Learner pattern | Diagnosis | Suggested feedback | Retest action |
|---|---|---|---|
| “KSR makes this obvious” | Uses case name as conclusion | “KSR allows flexible reasoning, but it still needs a reason. Pick one: design need, market pressure, predictable result, finite options, or known substitution.” | Complete fact-bank mini-drill. |
| “No single reference, so nonobvious” | Confuses §102 and §103 | “No single reference may help novelty, but §103 can combine references if a PHOSITA had a reason to combine.” | Write Graham table with A/B references. |
| “It looks simple” | Personal intuition / hindsight | “Replace your intuition with a pre-filing PHOSITA fact. What would ordinary skill at that time have taught?” | Identify time, field, problem, and prior-art reason. |
| Ignores counterargument | One-sided analysis | “A stronger §103 answer gives both sides. Add teaching away, unpredictability, or missing motivation if facts support it.” | Add one anti-obviousness paragraph. |
