# Day 5 Feedback Samples

| Learner pattern | Diagnosis | Suggested feedback | Retest action |
|---|---|---|---|
| “One example supports all variants” | Ignores claim scope | “先比较 example 和 claim scope。一个例子可能不支持整个 genus，尤其在不可预测领域。” | Complete Broad Claim Drill A. |
| Uses possession and enablement interchangeably | Collapses §112 doctrines | “Written description 问申请日是否显示 possession；enablement 问 PHOSITA 能否实施 full scope without undue experimentation。” | Fill Day 5 calibration table again. |
| Mentions full scope but no facts | Thin application | “请列出 representative species、common structural features、screening guidance、predictability 中哪些存在或缺失。” | Add fact list under WD and enablement. |
| Ignores definiteness | Misses reasonable certainty issue | “如果 claim term 范围不清，也可能有 definiteness 问题。请问 term 是否让 PHOSITA reasonably certain。” | Add definiteness sentence. |
