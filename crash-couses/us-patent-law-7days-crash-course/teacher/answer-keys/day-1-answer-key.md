# Day 1 Answer Key

## Private scoring notes

Expected elements:
- Patent as right to exclude, not affirmative right to practice.
- Patent bargain: disclosure in exchange for time-limited exclusivity.
- Utility / design / plant distinction.
- Provisional vs nonprovisional distinction: provisional not examined and not itself issued as patent.
- Claims define legal boundary; specification supports and informs.

Strong answer pattern:
1. Identify the type of protection.
2. State what cannot yet be concluded.
3. Tie claim boundary to later patentability and infringement analysis.

Common failures:
- Says patent means permission to make product.
- Treats design and utility as interchangeable.
- Treats provisional as granted patent.
