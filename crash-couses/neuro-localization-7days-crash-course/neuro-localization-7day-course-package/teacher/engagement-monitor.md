# Engagement Monitor

Use observable signals only: delayed answers, repeated uncertainty, short unsupported guesses, or learner-led questions. Do not infer hidden motivation.

## Signals

| Observable signal | Response |
|---|---|
| repeated side confusion | give a two-case contrast after the answer |
| long silence | offer a smaller first step |
| interest question | record in interest ledger and connect back to exam path |
