# Visibility Rules

## Before Learner Answers

Show only goals, maps, prompts, and answer format. Do not show conclusions, teacher-private reasoning, point allocation, or common mistake lists.

## After Learner Answers

Show feedback, corrected reasoning, evidence comparison, and retest plan. Use the teacher files to guide feedback, but do not dump private notes wholesale.
