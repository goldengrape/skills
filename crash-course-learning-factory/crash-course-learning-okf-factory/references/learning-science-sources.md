# Sources

## Retrieval Practice

Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006). Test-enhanced learning.  
https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x

Use for: making active recall and short quizzes central to daily sessions.

## Effective Learning Techniques

Dunlosky, J., Rawson, K. A., Marsh, E. J., Nathan, M. J., & Willingham, D. T. (2013). Improving students' learning with effective learning techniques.  
https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266

Use for: practice testing, distributed practice, self-explanation, interleaving.

## Feedback

Hattie, J., & Timperley, H. (2007). The power of feedback.  
https://doi.org/10.3102/003465430298487

Use for: answer-level feedback that identifies scoring points, missing points, and next actions.

## Cognitive Load

Sweller, J. (1988). Cognitive load during problem solving: Effects on learning.  
https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1202_4

Use for: keeping each one-hour session small, sequenced, and example-rich.

## ICAP Framework

Chi, M. T. H., & Wylie, R. (2014). The ICAP Framework.  
https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2014.965823

Use for: moving the learner from passive listening to constructive explanation and interactive dialogue.
