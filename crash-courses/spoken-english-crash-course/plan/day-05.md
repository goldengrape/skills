# Day 5 — Recommend a Course

## Language objective

Ask concise questions about needs and constraints, then make a conditional recommendation with reasons and one caveat.

## Topic mode

`anchored` · intent: `anchored_practice`

## Planned topic or evergreen fallback

Planned: recommend a continuing-education course to a busy adult. Fallback: recommend a learning format—self-paced, live online, or in person.

## Topic adaptation card

- selection rule: use the planned role-play; switch to the fallback if naming a course creates unnecessary knowledge load
- topic-fit checks: elicits interaction and recommendation; fictional details protect privacy; no specialist knowledge required; balances recent AI topics; setup under one minute
- evergreen fallback: recommend one of three learning formats
- load-reduction fallback: ask only about goal and weekly time, then recommend one format
- switch trigger: course details block interaction after the coach offers simple fictional choices
- preserve after switching: keep needs questions, conditional recommendation, reasons, and caveat

## Scenario and roles

The coach is a busy adult with a goal, schedule, and budget. The learner is an adviser who must ask questions before recommending.

## Time budget

- 0:00–1:00 confirm role-play
- 1:00–3:00 retrieve one trade-off phrase and prepare two questions
- 3:00–10:00 needs interview and recommendation
- 10:00–13:00 repair and changed-constraint variation
- 13:00–15:00 recap and record
- optional 5-minute extension: handle one objection

## Recall cue

Retrieve: `It depends on...` or `The main trade-off is...`. Add: `What matters most to you?` and `I would recommend it if...`.

## Opening question

Coach begins: “I want to learn a new professional skill, but I only have three hours a week. What should I choose?”

## Main speaking task

Ask at least two needs questions. Recommend one option with two reasons, one caveat, and a condition. Then respond when the coach changes one constraint.

## Optional live micro-correction targets

Question formation, polite register, recommendation frames, and conditionals with `if`.

## Learner-output target and coach-turn limit

Learner: at least two questions, one 60-second recommendation, and one revised response. Coach: answer briefly, add one changed constraint, and avoid explaining the task during role-play.

## Fatigue or short-time fallback

Ask one goal question, recommend one learning format with `I would recommend... if...`, repair and repeat, then close.

## Repair and repetition

Choose one question or recommendation that would be more natural or precise. Give a one-breath cue and have the learner reuse it after the constraint changes.

## Transfer question

“How would your recommendation change if the person cared more about feedback than flexibility?”

## Interest evidence to capture

Record whether the learner explicitly wants more continuing-education scenarios. Do not treat role-play success or difficulty alone as affinity evidence.

## Language evidence to capture

Record question clarity, whether the recommendation followed the stated needs, conditional language, caveat, and reuse after correction.

## Closeout instruction

Ask for a 20-second final recommendation. Complete the record and state patch, then set exactly one next action: transfer the recommendation structure to a new practical problem on Day 6.
