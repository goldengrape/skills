# Week 1 Mission

## Learner goal

Build a sustainable daily speaking habit through short ChatGPT Live conversations. By the end of the week, the learner should be able to explain a process, compare two options, and give a qualified recommendation with less planning time and more connected speech.

## Primary language targets

1. **Scenario/function:** explain a process and give a recommendation that another person can act on.
2. **Fluency/discourse:** build 45–90 seconds of connected speech using signposts, examples, and follow-up answers.
3. **Language repair:** qualify comparisons and opinions with accurate, natural phrases such as `It depends on...`, `One advantage is...`, `The main trade-off is...`, and `I would recommend it if...`.

These are provisional targets based on the learner's goal and evidence gaps. The week diagnoses actual recurring issues instead of inventing them.

## Topic policy

- mode: guided adaptive
- anchored days: 1, 2, 3, and 5
- adaptive days: 4, 6, and 7
- current events: off; no future news item is built into the pack
- AI and adult continuing education remain `possible` until explicit preference or repeated topic-affinity evidence supports promotion
- a topic may change during a session, but the language objective must remain fixed
- difficulty, tiredness, weak background knowledge, or a vague prompt must not be recorded as topic dislike

## Learner controls

During Live practice, the learner may say: **more correction**, **less correction**, **explain**, **skip**, or **wrap up**. The current request overrides the saved correction preference for that session.
