# Final Review Mock Exam — Teacher Key

## Part A — Terms, 20

Award up to 5 per term:

- 2 for accurate definition.
- 1 for placing it in the patent system.
- 1 for distinguishing adjacent concept.
- 1 for one example or misuse warning.

## Part B — Comparison, 15

Award:

- 5 for defining both sides.
- 5 for explaining the legal question each side asks.
- 3 for showing how they interact in one fact pattern.
- 2 for a common confusion warning.

## Part C — Issue Spotting, 30

Expected analysis:

- §101: possible abstract idea / data processing issue; ordinary server and generic alert are risk facts; technical improvement facts would matter.
- §102: prior patent has data collection and alert but lacks exact statistical method; single-reference anticipation may fail unless another reference discloses all claim elements.
- §103: prior patent plus journal article raises combination issue; discuss PHOSITA, motivation, predictable use in maintenance context, and counterarguments.
- §112: one working example vs broad all-industrial-equipment claim raises enablement and written description concerns; claim scope matters.
- Claim construction: terms such as “statistical method,” “industrial equipment,” and “alert” may change validity and infringement analysis.

## Part D — Source Control, 10

Expected analysis:

- MPEP is USPTO examination guidance, not the highest legal authority and not identical to binding judicial precedent.
- Check statute / case law, MPEP version and cutoff, subsequent USPTO publications, Federal Register updates, and current USPTO pages for dynamic topics.
