# Learning Control Policy

## Default target

A-priority concepts target L6. B-priority concepts usually target L3–L4 unless learner interest or exam evidence raises their importance.

## Productive friction

Preserve independent recall, route selection, misuse discrimination, and transfer. Remove only tool friction such as formatting, file organization, or source indexing.

## Assistance modes

- guided: teacher explains or heavily scaffolds.
- semi_guided: learner chooses with limited hints.
- blind: learner answers without hints.
- barehand: no notes, no hints, cross-day mixed context.

## Evidence rules

- L6 requires misuse discrimination or flawed-answer checks.
- L7 requires transfer into a new fact pattern.
- barehand checkpoints should appear every 2-3 learning days when feasible.
- Feedback must cite source, rubric, or learner output evidence.

## Model-vs-reality protocol

Distinguish the textbook exam model from real-world legal complexity. The course can teach a simplified exam route, but must say when real cases require lawyer review, current USPTO guidance, or fuller factual investigation.

## Negative feature list

Do not use badge, streak, ranking, or leaderboard mechanics as evidence of learning. They may be mentioned only as low-value gamification to avoid.
