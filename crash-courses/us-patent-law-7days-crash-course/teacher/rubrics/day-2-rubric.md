# Day 2 Rubric

| Dimension | Points | Criteria |
|---|---:|---|
| Term precision | 5 | Uses the correct legal vocabulary for: statutory category、judicial exception、Alice/Mayo Step 2A / Step 2B、abstract idea、natural law. Does not collapse adjacent concepts. |
| Rule structure | 5 | States the legal question before applying facts. Keeps the day’s doctrinal gate separate. |
| Source / case awareness | 5 | Connects at least one statute, MPEP topic, or case card to the analysis without overclaiming authority. |
| Fact application | 5 | Applies rule to concrete facts, not only definitions. |
| Uncertainty and boundary control | 5 | States what cannot yet be concluded and what further fact/source check would be needed. |

## Assistance adjustment

| Mode | Cap for evidence level |
|---|---|
| blind | Full credit available; may support L6 if answer distinguishes misuse. |
| semi_guided | Max 20 for quiz score; max L5 evidence unless retested. |
| guided | Max 15 for quiz score; no L6 evidence. |
| copied / searched | Record as exposure only; no mastery upgrade. |

## Red flags

- Uses commercial value as substitute for legal analysis.
- Gives real legal advice for a real invention.
- Treats MPEP, casebook, and statute as identical authority.
- Skips claim language when claim boundary matters.

## Darwin R2 scoring refinement

§101 gate separation: awards up to 5 fact-application points only if the learner does not use novelty or obviousness as the eligibility answer. Strong answers mention statutory category, possible judicial exception, and additional elements/practical application in that order.
