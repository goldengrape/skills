# Day 5 Answer Key

## Private scoring notes

Expected elements:
- Written description: possession of claimed invention as of filing.
- Enablement: ability to make and use full scope without undue experimentation.
- Definiteness: reasonable certainty of claim scope.
- Claim scope matters; broad genus claims require support across scope.
- Amgen v. Sanofi warning for broad functional genus claims.

Strong answer pattern:
1. Identify which §112 requirement is implicated.
2. Tie the requirement to claim scope.
3. Compare examples in spec against breadth of claim.
4. Avoid collapsing written description into enablement.

Common failures:
- “One example enables everything.”
- Treats inventor possession and making/using as same question.
- Ignores claim clarity.

## Feedback samples

## Darwin R2 calibration expectations

The calibration table should distinguish:

- Written description: possession of the claimed invention as of filing; check representative disclosure, common features, and support for claim breadth.
- Enablement: ability to make and use the full scope without undue experimentation; check predictability, guidance, working examples, and experimentation burden.
- Definiteness: reasonable certainty of claim scope; check ambiguous functional or relative terms.
