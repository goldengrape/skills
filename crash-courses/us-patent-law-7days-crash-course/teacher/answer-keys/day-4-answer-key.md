# Day 4 Answer Key

## Private scoring notes

Expected elements:
- Identify PHOSITA perspective at relevant time.
- Apply Graham factors.
- Use KSR flexible approach without making it conclusory.
- Explain motivation to combine, predictable results, design need, market pressure, teaching away, or secondary considerations when facts support them.
- Avoid hindsight.

Strong answer pattern:
1. State prior art and differences.
2. Explain what PHOSITA would know.
3. Give reason for or against combination.
4. Address at least one counterargument.

Common failures:
- “It is obvious because it looks simple.”
- “No single reference, therefore nonobvious.”
- Invokes KSR as a shortcut without reasoned explanation.

## Feedback samples

| Learner pattern | Suggested feedback |
|---|---|
| Correct rule but thin facts | “规则方向对了。现在把 claim element 或事实逐项接上，避免只背规则。” |
| Confuses adjacent gates | “先暂停。你正在用一个 gate 回答另一个 gate。请先写出本题只问什么。” |
| Overconfident yes/no | “结论可以暂放后面。先列出支持、反对和不确定事实。” |
| Needs hints | “记录 assistance mode；提示后作答不能直接升级到独立掌握。” |


## Darwin R2 calibration expectations

The PHOSITA profile should include field, relevant time, ordinary skill, and known design problem. A strong answer does not merely name KSR; it identifies a fact-supported reason to combine, such as design need, market pressure, predictable result, finite options, known substitution, or a record-supported common-sense rationale.

The answer should also give one counterweight when facts permit: teaching away, incompatibility, unpredictability, missing reason, or secondary considerations tied to the claimed feature.
