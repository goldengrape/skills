# Day 6 Answer Key

## Private scoring notes

Expected elements:
- Claim construction before infringement.
- Intrinsic evidence: claim language, specification, prosecution history.
- Literal infringement requires every limitation.
- Doctrine of equivalents has limits and cannot erase claim elements.
- Remedies: eBay rejects automatic injunction; Halo concerns enhanced damages in egregious cases.

Strong answer pattern:
1. Interpret disputed term.
2. Apply interpreted claim to accused product.
3. Separate literal infringement from equivalents.
4. Discuss remedy cautiously.

Common failures:
- Similar product = infringement.
- Equivalents used as free-floating fairness test.
- Victory = automatic injunction or treble damages.
