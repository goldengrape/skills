# Day 7 Answer Key

## Private scoring notes

Expected elements:
- PTAB / IPR / PGR are post-grant mechanisms, not ordinary prosecution.
- IPR commonly centers on §§102/103 using patents and printed publications.
- PGR can be broader but is timing-limited.
- AI-assisted inventions require separating eligibility, inventorship, disclosure, and obviousness.
- Design patent issues are not the same as utility patent function claims.
- Dynamic materials: fees, MPEP updates, SME guidance, AI guidance.

Strong answer pattern:
1. Place PTAB in lifecycle.
2. Separate AI-related legal questions.
3. Avoid giving real-client legal advice.
4. Identify what must be checked in current USPTO materials.

Common failures:
- Treats AI participation as automatic bar to patentability.
- Treats IPR as general invalidity lawsuit.
- Applies utility patent tests directly to design patent visual subject matter.
