# Diagram Quality Rules

Every generated diagram must check:

- title is clear
- labels are readable
- axis or flow direction is explicit when applicable
- no legal relationship is reversed
- source and generation date are recorded in `assets/diagrams/index.md`
- diagram is not used as a substitute for legal analysis

For non-quantitative legal workflows, “axis” may mean the reading direction or decision order.
