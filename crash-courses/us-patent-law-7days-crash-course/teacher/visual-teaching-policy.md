# Visual Teaching Policy

Use visual aids for workflows, decision trees, claim structure, and process comparison when text alone becomes heavy.

## Priority

1. Prefer generated Python / matplotlib diagrams when a simple workflow, tree, table-like map, or process figure can be made accurately.
2. Use authoritative external or open-source diagrams when the legal or procedural image is complex and already maintained by a trusted source.
3. Restrict ASCII diagrams to very small sketches. Do not rely on ASCII for complex legal workflow diagrams.

## Patent law visual triggers

- patentability funnel
- claim anatomy
- application-to-grant process
- PTAB post-grant path
- source hierarchy
