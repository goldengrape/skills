# Compressed Notes

## One-page path

```text
1. What is the claimed invention?
2. What type of patent or application posture is involved?
3. §101: Is the subject matter eligible?
4. §102: Is every claim element disclosed in one prior art reference?
5. §103: Would PHOSITA find the claimed difference obvious?
6. §112: Does the specification support, enable, and clearly claim the invention?
7. Claim construction: What do the claim words mean?
8. Infringement: Does the accused product or method meet every limitation?
9. Remedies / PTAB: What happens after enforcement or grant?
```

## Core contrasts

| Contrast | Key distinction |
|---|---|
| §101 vs §102 | Eligibility asks whether this kind of subject matter can be patented; novelty asks whether it was already disclosed. |
| §102 vs §103 | §102 usually focuses on one reference disclosing every element; §103 can use combinations and PHOSITA reasoning. |
| Written description vs enablement | Possession at filing vs ability to make and use without undue experimentation. |
| Claim construction vs infringement | First interpret the claim; then compare accused product or method. |
| IPR vs PGR | Both are PTAB trials, but grounds and timing differ. |
