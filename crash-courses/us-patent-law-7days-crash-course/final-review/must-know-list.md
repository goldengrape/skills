# Must-Know List

## A topics

1. Patent right is mainly a right to exclude.
2. Utility patent is the main route for functional inventions; design patent protects ornamental design.
3. Provisional application does not itself become a patent.
4. Claim language defines legal boundaries.
5. §101 categories: process, machine, manufacture, composition of matter.
6. Judicial exceptions: natural laws, natural phenomena, abstract ideas.
7. Alice/Mayo path: exception plus additional elements analysis.
8. Prior art feeds both §102 and §103.
9. Anticipation usually requires one reference to disclose every claim element.
10. PHOSITA is a legal construct, not the inventor personally.
11. Graham factors organize obviousness facts.
12. KSR made obviousness more flexible but not purely intuitive.
13. Written description asks whether the inventor had possession.
14. Enablement asks whether PHOSITA can make and use without undue experimentation.
15. Definiteness asks whether claim scope is reasonably certain.
16. Claim construction comes before many validity and infringement disputes.
17. Direct infringement requires every claim limitation.
18. Doctrine of equivalents cannot erase claim limits.
19. eBay changed the old assumption about automatic injunctions.
20. IPR and PGR are important post-grant challenge paths.

## Source discipline

- Use 35 U.S.C. for statutes.
- Use 37 C.F.R. for rules.
- Use MPEP for USPTO examination guidance.
- Use cases for judicial boundaries.
- Use USPTO / Federal Register for recent guidance.
