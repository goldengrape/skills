# Visual Teaching Policy

Prefer Python/matplotlib generated diagrams for simple pathway maps and comparison graphics. Use authoritative open-source diagrams when anatomy is too complex to redraw accurately. Avoid ASCII diagrams for spatial localization because they become misleading.

Every diagram must have a course use, source status, and quality note in `assets/diagrams/index.md`.
