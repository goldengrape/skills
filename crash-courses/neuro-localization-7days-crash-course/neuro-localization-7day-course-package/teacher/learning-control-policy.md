# Learning Control Policy

## Target

A-priority concepts target L6 by default. L6 requires misuse discrimination: the learner must identify a flawed localization and explain why it fails. L7 requires transfer to a new mixed case.

## Assistance Modes

Use guided, semi_guided, blind, and barehand modes. Preserve independent recall before giving feedback. Schedule barehand checks every 2-3 sessions.

## Feedback Anchoring

Anchor feedback to source material, rubric criteria, and the learner's actual output. Distinguish the exam/textbook model from real-world clinical complexity.

## Negative Features

Do not use badge, streak, leaderboard, 排名, 连续打卡, or 徽章 mechanics as evidence of learning.
