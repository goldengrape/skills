# Time Policy

## soft

Default. Daily time is approximately 60 minutes. The teacher may extend slightly for repair, or compress B/C material to protect A concepts.

## strict

Use only if the learner requests it. Stop at the time limit, update unfinished items in `state/next-action.md`, and resume next time.
