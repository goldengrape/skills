# Visibility Rules

## Before Learner Answers

- Show only the prompt, neutral instructions and allowed context.
- Do not show rubrics, answer keys, hidden scoring notes, or expected answer elements.
- Do not leak teacher_thinks.

## After Learner Answers

- Give score and reason.
- Identify correct parts and missing parts.
- Correct misconceptions.
- Provide a revised version that can be used for study.
- Update state files.
