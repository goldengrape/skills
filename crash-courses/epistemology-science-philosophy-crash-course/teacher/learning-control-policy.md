# Learning Control Policy

## Assistance modes

Use guided, semi_guided, blind and barehand modes. Record assistance mode and score_type in score history.

## Evidence rules

- L6 requires misuse discrimination: the learner must detect and repair flawed statements.
- L7 requires transfer: the learner must apply a concept to a new case and state the boundary.
- Keep independent recall before explanation when checking prior learning.
- Feedback must cite the source or 资料 basis inside the course package, such as glossary, plan, or teacher rubric.
- Distinguish textbook / 考试 simplified formulations from real philosophical debates when needed.

## Negative feature list

Do not use badge, streak, leaderboard or ranking as proof of learning. Use evidence from answers and repairs.
