# Day 5 Answer Key

Teacher-private. Do not show before learner answers.

## Prompts covered

- 名词解释: 解释可证伪性。
- 简答: 为什么归纳主义会遇到休谟问题？
- 判断并修正: “波普尔认为科学理论一旦被证伪就彻底没价值。”请判断并改写。
- 小案例: 为什么“任何事情都能被解释成支持这个理论”会削弱科学性？

## Evaluation notes

Use the day rubric and course glossary. Reward careful boundary statements. Penalize overstrong claims such as reducing a philosopher to a slogan.
