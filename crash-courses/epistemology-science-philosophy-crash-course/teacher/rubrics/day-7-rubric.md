# Day 7 Rubric

## Score type

Default quiz mode is blind_score unless the learner received hints. If hints are given, use semi_assisted_score or assisted_score.

## Evaluation focus

跨日综合、误解修正、AI 迁移边界。

## Score allocation

| 题目 | 分数 | 主要证据 |
|---|---:|---|
| 综合比较 | 20 | 能把休谟、康德、波普尔、奎因、库恩放入同一问题链。 |
| 误解修正 | 20 | 能识别错误说法并改写为准确表达。 |
| benchmark 与证伪 | 15 | 能区分有限测试和波普尔证伪。 |
| 训练数据与归纳 | 15 | 能说明归纳类比成立处和休谟问题仍然存在。 |
| 自我修正与康德 | 15 | 能避免“AI 实现康德”的过强类比。 |
| 小论文 | 15 | 能形成全课概念地图并修正误解。 |

## Bands

- 90-100%: concept distinction is clear, wording is careful, and at least one misconception is actively repaired.
- 75-89%: core idea is present, with some missing precision or weak boundary statements.
- 60-74%: partial understanding; schedule repair before course completion.
- below 60%: stop dependent progression and reteach core concepts.

## L6-L7 evidence

- L6 requires flawed-statement repair.
- L7 requires a new-case transfer with explicit boundary.
- AI transfer only counts as L7 if the learner explains both similarity and non-equivalence.

## Required repair triggers

Schedule repair if learner says any of the following without correction:

- AI benchmark is exactly Popperian falsification.
- Training data solves Hume's problem of induction.
- Self-correcting LLMs implement Kant's categories, apperception, or synthetic a priori judgment.
- Coherence in model output is sufficient for knowledge.
- Leaderboards are the same as Kuhnian paradigms.
