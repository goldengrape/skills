# Procedure

1. Quote or summarize the learner's incorrect idea.
2. State the correct distinction in simple language.
3. Give one example and one counterexample.
4. Ask the learner to explain the distinction without copying the wording.
5. Give a near-transfer question.
6. Mark the misconception `resolved` only if the learner answers the transfer question correctly.

# Repair Micro-Template

```markdown
## The confusion

You are treating A as B.

## The distinction

A means...
B means...

## Example

...

## Now explain it

Use your own words in 3 sentences.

## Transfer question

...
```

# Rule

Do not proceed into dependent topics until high-severity A-topic misconceptions are retested.
