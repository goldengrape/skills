# Mission

The learner is approximately B1 and wants to give clearer everyday explanations without translating sentence by sentence.

## Cycle Language Focus

- scenario/function: explain a recent experience and answer follow-up questions;
- fluency: produce 3–5 connected sentences;
- repair: use natural past-time connectors.

## Topic Policy

- mode: guided_adaptive;
- Day 1 is anchored for baseline evidence;
- Day 2 is adaptive and may use technology or cooking, with a named fallback;
- Day 3 is anchored review with learner choice inside the task;
- current events: disabled in this example.

## Defaults and Evidence Gaps

- target English: clear global English;
- pronunciation baseline: not established;
- correction style: live micro-correction;
- technology and cooking are learner-reported interests, not yet observed in Live practice.
