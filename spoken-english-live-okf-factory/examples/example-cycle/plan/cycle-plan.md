# Cycle Plan

| Day | Scenario | Main task | Repair or transfer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weekend recap | Tell one short story | Repair past-time connectors |
| 2 | Explain a small problem | Describe cause, effect, and response | Reuse connectors under follow-up questions |
| 3 | Free experience story | Tell and defend a choice | Transfer phrases and review cycle |
