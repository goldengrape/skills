# Cycle Plan

| Day | Language objective | Topic mode | Planned or fallback topic | Main task |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tell a short past event with connected sequencing | anchored | Weekend recap | Tell one story and answer follow-ups |
| 2 | Explain cause, response, and result | adaptive | Fallback: a small everyday problem | Select technology, cooking, or fallback and explain a problem |
| 3 | Justify a decision and transfer expressions | anchored review | A recent decision chosen by the learner | Tell, defend, and restate the decision |

The topic may change on Day 2, but the explanation objective remains fixed. The adaptive intent is `test_possible`; a later cycle should use `deepen_confirmed` and `refresh_variety` deliberately rather than repeatedly selecting the easiest confirmed interest.
