# Day 2 — Explain a Small Problem

## Goal
Explain what went wrong, why, and what you did next.

## Scenario and roles
The coach is a colleague asking about a delayed task.

## Time budget
15 minutes.

## Recall cue
Retrieve one connector from Day 1.

## Opening question
Can you tell me about a small problem you solved recently?

## Main speaking task
Give the sequence, cause, response, and result.

## Optional live micro-correction targets
“Because of,” “so,” “in the end,” and natural work-safe wording.

## Learner-output target and coach-turn limit
Learner speaks for roughly two-thirds or more of the session. Coach turns stay to one question, follow-up, or correction cue.

## Fatigue or short-time fallback
Continue the current scenario with one final answer, make at most one useful repair, then close out.

## Repair and repetition
Restate the clearest two-sentence version.

## Transfer question
Explain the same issue to a customer rather than a colleague.

## Evidence to capture
Connector retrieval and adaptation to audience.

## Closeout instruction
Produce the session record and one next action.
